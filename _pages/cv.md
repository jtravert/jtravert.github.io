I completed [my PhD in engineer sciences](http://jtravert.github.io/assets/files/Travert_PhD_Manuscript.pdf) in October 2025, which took place at [LHSV](https://lhsv.ip-paris.fr/), and at [EDF R&D](https://www.edf.fr/en/the-edf-group/who-we-are/activities/research-and-development), Chatou.


I was supervised by [Sébastien Boyaval](https://lhsv.ip-paris.fr/membres/sebastien-boyaval) from [LHSV](https://lhsv.ip-paris.fr/) and [Inria](https://www.inria.fr/fr/matherials), and of [Cédric Goeury](https://lhsv.ip-paris.fr/membres/cedric-goeury), Vito Bacchi, and Fabrice Zaoui from [EDF R&D](https://www.edf.fr/en/the-edf-group/who-we-are/activities/research-and-development).

I graduated from [Ense3](https://ense3.grenoble-inp.fr/) with a specialization in hydraulics and environmental engineering in 2023.

During my studies, I did a 6-month research internship under the supervision of Vito Bacchi and Florent Taccone from [EDF R&D](https://www.edf.fr/en/the-edf-group/who-we-are/activities/research-and-development). I was working on modeling of extreme flood events in large watersheds using [openTELEMAC suite of solvers](https://www.opentelemac.org/).

I also had the opportunity to carry out a 3-month research internship at [Inrae](https://www.inrae.fr/) under the supervision of [Alain Recking](https://scholar.google.fr/citations?user=ZPtDX1gAAAAJ&hl=fr). During this academic internship, I was working on the estimation of sediment transport in Alpine rivers through field campaigns and documenting and developing a numerical code for erosion on watershed during torrential floods.

For more details, see my [resume](http://jtravert.github.io/assets/pdf/cv_TRAVERT.pdf).
