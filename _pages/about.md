Since December 2025, I am a Research Engineer at **EDF R&D**, working in the
National Laboratory for Hydraulics and the Environment department.

In October 2025, I completed my PhD in engineer sciences on the **Calibration
of roughness for flood modeling using satellite data** under the supervision of
[Sébastien Boyaval](https://lhsv.ip-paris.fr/membres/sebastien-boyaval) from
[LHSV](https://lhsv.ip-paris.fr/) and
[Inria](https://www.inria.fr/fr/matherials), and of
[Cédric Goeury](https://lhsv.ip-paris.fr/membres/cedric-goeury), Vito Bacchi, and
Fabrice Zaoui from EDF R&D.

I now contribute to projects on the evaluation of water resources, with a
specific interest in the development of numerical tools.
