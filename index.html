Jean-Paul Travert

Research engineer at EDF R&D

Since December 2025, I am a Research Engineer at EDF R&D, working in the National Laboratory for Hydraulics and the Environment department.

In October 2025, I completed my PhD in engineer sciences on the Calibration of roughness for flood modeling using satellite data under the supervision of Sébastien Boyaval from LHSV and Inria, and of Cédric Goeury, Vito Bacchi, and Fabrice Zaoui from EDF R&D.

I now contribute to projects on the evaluation of water resources, with a specific interest in the development of numerical tools.
